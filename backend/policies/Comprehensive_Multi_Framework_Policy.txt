INFORMATION SECURITY AND DATA PRIVACY POLICY

Document Owner: Chief Information Security Officer
Version: 2.1
Effective Date: 1 April 2026
Next Scheduled Review: 1 April 2027
Classification: Internal / Public Summary

================================================================
1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
================================================================

This policy establishes NovaCloud Technologies Pvt. Ltd.'s ("NovaCloud", "we", "us")
commitments regarding the collection, processing, storage, and protection of
personal data belonging to our customers, employees, and business partners, and
sets out the security controls we maintain over the systems that process that
data. It applies to all employees, contractors, and third-party service
providers who access NovaCloud systems or data, and to all products and
services offered by NovaCloud, including our payment processing module.

This information security policy has been formally approved by the Board of
Directors and is communicated to all staff at induction and via annual
refresher training. The policy is reviewed on an annual basis, or sooner if
required by a material change in applicable law or a significant security
incident, through a documented management review process.

================================================================
2. GOVERNANCE AND ACCOUNTABILITY
================================================================

NovaCloud has appointed a dedicated Data Protection Officer (DPO) responsible
for overseeing compliance with this policy and applicable data protection
law. The DPO can be contacted at dpo@novacloud.example for any privacy-related
query, complaint, or exercise of rights described in Section 6.

Segregation of duties is maintained between the engineering team that
provisions production access, the security team that reviews and approves
access requests, and the internal audit function that periodically verifies
that access continues to be appropriate. No single individual can both
request and approve their own elevated access.

================================================================
3. LAWFUL BASIS, CONSENT, AND NOTICE
================================================================

Where NovaCloud relies on consent as the lawful basis for processing, we
obtain explicit, informed consent through a clear affirmative action (a
double opt-in checkbox during account creation) before any non-essential
processing begins. Consent language is written in plain, unambiguous terms
and a complete record of consent (timestamp, IP address, and the exact
notice text shown) is retained for audit purposes. Users can withdraw
consent at any time through their account settings; withdrawal takes effect
within 24 hours and does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out
before withdrawal.

Where consent is not the basis relied upon, NovaCloud processes personal
data only where necessary for the performance of a contract, compliance
with a legal obligation, or our legitimate interest in operating and
securing our services, and this basis is documented per processing activity
in our internal Record of Processing Activities.

A privacy notice, available at novacloud.example/privacy, is provided to
every data subject before or at the point of data collection. It sets out
the purpose of processing, the categories of data collected, the applicable
retention period, and a description of data principal rights, together with
DPO contact details.

================================================================
4. PURPOSE LIMITATION AND DATA MINIMIZATION
================================================================

Personal data is collected only for the specified, legitimate purpose
disclosed at the point of collection and is not used for any secondary
purpose without obtaining additional consent or establishing a separate
lawful basis. Before any new data field is added to a collection form, the
requesting team must document why that field is necessary and adequate for
the stated purpose; fields judged not relevant to the purpose are rejected
at design review. A quarterly data minimization review is conducted by the
security team to identify and remove any fields that have become
unnecessary.

================================================================
5. DATA RETENTION AND DELETION
================================================================

Personal data is retained only for as long as necessary to fulfil the
purpose for which it was collected, subject to any longer retention period
mandated by law (for example, financial transaction records, which are
retained for the statutory period applicable to tax and audit
requirements). Our standard retention period for customer account data is
three (3) years after the last active interaction, after which the data is
subject to deletion after purpose has been fulfilled, applying the
principle of storage limitation. Backups containing deleted personal data
are purged from backup media within 90 days of the live deletion.

================================================================
6. DATA PRINCIPAL / DATA SUBJECT RIGHTS
================================================================

Individuals have the right to access a copy of the personal data we hold
about them, the right to correction of inaccurate data, and the right to
erasure of their data where it is no longer needed for the purpose
collected or where consent has been withdrawn. Requests can be submitted
via privacy@novacloud.example and are actioned within 15 days. Where a
request cannot be honoured (for example, where data must be retained for a
legal obligation), the individual is informed of the reason in writing.

Complaints that are not resolved to the data subject's satisfaction may be
escalated to our designated grievance officer at grievance@novacloud.example.
The grievance officer acknowledges every complaint within 48 hours and
provides a substantive response, including next steps and an expected
timeline, within 15 days of receipt.

================================================================
7. SECURITY SAFEGUARDS
================================================================

7.1 Encryption. All personal data is protected using encryption both at
rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.2 or higher). Secure transmission is
enforced for all external API endpoints; connections that cannot negotiate
an approved TLS version are rejected. Cardholder data specifically is
additionally protected through masking on display and tokenization at
capture, so that raw card numbers are never stored in application
databases.

7.2 Access control. Access to production systems is governed by a
role-based access control model implementing the principle of least
privilege. Every account is uniquely identified; shared or generic
credentials are prohibited. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory
for all administrative access and for any access to systems processing
personal or cardholder data. Passwords must meet a minimum complexity
standard, and interactive sessions are subject to an automatic logoff after
15 minutes of inactivity. An emergency access ("break glass") procedure
exists for exceptional situations and is itself logged and reviewed.

7.3 Network security. Production environments sit behind a firewall with
documented inbound and outbound rule sets; network segmentation isolates
the cardholder data environment from the general corporate network.
Firewall rules are reviewed quarterly.

7.4 Asset management. NovaCloud maintains an asset inventory covering all
hardware, software, and data assets in scope of this policy, classified by
sensitivity, and reviewed twice yearly to ensure it remains current.

7.5 Vulnerability and patch management. Systems are subject to monthly
vulnerability scans and an annual third-party penetration test.
Vulnerabilities rated critical or high are remediated through our patch
management process within 14 days of disclosure; a documented secure
coding standard governs in-house development, including mandatory code
review before any change reaches production.

7.6 Backup and resilience. Production databases are backed up daily, with
backup restoration tested quarterly to confirm recovery objectives can be
met. A documented business continuity plan defines recovery time and
recovery point objectives for critical systems.

================================================================
8. MONITORING, LOGGING, AND AUDIT
================================================================

Access to systems handling personal or cardholder data is recorded through
centralized audit logs, including successful and failed authentication
attempts, administrative actions, and data export events. Logs are
retained for 12 months and are subject to continuous monitoring, with
automated anomaly detection alerting the security team to unusual access
patterns for further investigation. Log integrity is protected against
tampering through write-once storage.

================================================================
9. INCIDENT AND BREACH MANAGEMENT
================================================================

NovaCloud maintains a documented incident response plan covering
detection, containment, eradication, and recovery. In the event of a
confirmed personal data breach, the incident response team assesses the
risk to affected individuals and, where required, notifies the relevant
supervisory authority without undue delay, and in any event within 72
hours of becoming aware of the breach. Affected individuals are notified
directly where the breach is likely to result in a high risk to their
rights and freedoms. A post-incident review is conducted after every
significant event to capture lessons learned and update this policy where
necessary.

================================================================
10. THIRD-PARTY AND SUPPLY CHAIN RISK
================================================================

Vendors and sub-processors who handle personal data on NovaCloud's behalf
are subject to a data processing agreement and a pre-onboarding security
review appropriate to the sensitivity of the data involved. Critical
suppliers are reassessed annually against NovaCloud's supplier
cybersecurity requirements.

================================================================
11. CROSS-BORDER DATA TRANSFERS
================================================================

Where personal data is transferred outside the country in which it was
collected, NovaCloud applies appropriate safeguards, which may include
standard contractual clauses or reliance on an adequacy determination,
consistent with applicable data transfer restrictions. Certain categories
of data subject to local data localization requirements are retained on
servers within the country of collection.

================================================================
12. WORKFORCE SECURITY AWARENESS
================================================================

All employees complete mandatory security awareness training at induction
and annually thereafter, covering phishing recognition, safe data handling,
and incident reporting obligations. Periodic security reminders are
distributed via internal communications, and employees with access to
sensitive systems receive role-specific training in addition to the
general programme.

================================================================
13. POLICY REVIEW
================================================================

This policy is subject to management review on an annual cycle described
in Section 1. Material amendments are approved by the Chief Information
Security Officer and communicated to affected staff prior to taking
effect.
