ABC CORPORATION - IT SECURITY NOTES
===================================

We take security seriously. Staff should tell their manager if something goes
wrong with a computer. Our IT team looks into problems when they have time.

Logs are kept on each server for 30 days and then overwritten. We do not
retain logs for 180 days. Servers are hosted in a data centre in Singapore.

We do not report incidents to any outside agency. System clocks are set by
each administrator by hand.
