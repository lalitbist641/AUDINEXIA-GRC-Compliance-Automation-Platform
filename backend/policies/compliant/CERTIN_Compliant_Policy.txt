EXAMPLE CLOUD SERVICES PVT LTD - CERT-IN DIRECTIONS COMPLIANCE POLICY
=====================================================================
(Synthetic example for validating the CERT-In scan. Not legal advice.)

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
This policy implements the CERT-In Directions of 28 April 2022 issued under
section 70B(6) of the Information Technology Act, 2000. It applies to all ICT
systems operated by the company, which provides cloud service, virtual private
server and VPN services and handles virtual asset custody for some customers.

2. INCIDENT REPORTING
Every cyber incident is reported to CERT-In within 6 hours of being noticed or
brought to our notice, by email to incident@cert-in.org.in. The incident
reporting procedure names the Security Operations lead as the person who files
the report.

3. REPORTABLE INCIDENT TYPES
Our incident categories follow CERT-In Annexure I. They include unauthorised
access to systems or data, malicious code attacks such as malware, ransomware
and botnets, phishing and spoofing, denial of service and DDoS attacks, data
breach and data leak. All staff are trained to escalate these immediately.

4. POINT OF CONTACT
The Chief Information Security Officer is our designated point of contact for
CERT-In. Name, address, email and telephone number are shared with CERT-In and
are kept up to date whenever the role changes.

5. TIME SYNCHRONISATION
Clocks of all ICT systems are synchronised using the NTP server of the National
Informatics Centre (NIC) and the National Physical Laboratory (NPL), or servers
traceable to them.

6. LOG RETENTION
Logs of all ICT systems are retained for a rolling 180 days and stored within
Indian jurisdiction. Log retention is monitored and logs cannot be altered.

7. COOPERATION WITH CERT-IN
We comply with the CERT-In Directions. Logs and information requested by
CERT-In are provided on request and we cooperate with CERT-In during incident
response.

8. SUBSCRIBER RECORDS
We keep validated subscriber information, including the period of hire and the
IP addresses allotted, for 5 years after a subscription ends.

9. KYC AND TRANSACTION RECORDS
As a virtual asset service provider we maintain KYC records and financial
transaction records for 5 years.
